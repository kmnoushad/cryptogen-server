# NEXIO v6.8.1 candidate-survival hotfix

v6.8.1 fixes the observed 22-armed / 22-rejected Futures funnel without weakening the final entry, stop, R:R, BTC shock, account-risk, or Alpha protections.

## Futures candidate lifecycle

- Replaced the universal `0.45 ATR` breakout failure with setup-aware invalidation:
  - `FAST_BREAKOUT`: `0.45 ATR`, one closed-bar failure.
  - `STEADY_MOMENTUM`: `0.70 ATR`, two consecutive closed-bar failures.
  - `LIQUID_TREND`: `0.90 ATR`, two consecutive closed-bar failures.
- Detection ATR and invalidation level are frozen when a candidate arms.
- Retest support zones are setup-aware: fast remains strict while slow liquid trends can survive normal noise.
- Candidate lows are retained so a later structural stop cannot ignore an earlier post-arm low.
- Wave giveback now uses closed price, an ATR floor for small waves, setup-specific limits (50% / 60% / 70%), and consecutive confirmation for slow setups.

## Phase-aware manipulation handling

- `assessManipulationRisk` now separates `terminalRisk` from temporary `entryBlocked` conditions.
- Crash, taker-selling climax, severe OI divergence, catastrophic depth loss and depth-collapse-plus-spread-widening remain terminal.
- Temporary thin depth, spread or accumulated caution keeps the candidate alive but cannot FIRE until it recovers.
- Arm-time filtering remains strict.

## Reclaim and execution

- LIQUID_TREND confirmation now measures current EMA recovery instead of requiring all pre-retest 15m/30m heat to remain.
- Added `RECLAIMED_WAIT_BOOK`: a valid reclaim may wait up to three closed bars for execution quality to recover.
- Price cannot run more than `0.25 ATR` above the reclaim while waiting, and the reclaim structure must remain intact.
- Final spread, both-side depth, bid/ask imbalance, measured ask-ladder impact, OI, stop and net-R:R gates remain mandatory.
- Depth retention and spread expansion now compare against a rolling median of recent snapshots rather than one noisy prior snapshot.

## Diagnostics and persistence

- `/diagnostics` now presents the actual funnel: armed → retested → reclaimed → FIRE.
- Candidate terminal exits, waiting gates and pre-arm filters are shown separately so large impulse counts cannot hide rejection reasons.
- Every terminal candidate outcome is persisted to `nexio_events` as `FUTURES_CANDIDATE_OUTCOME` without a schema migration.

## Deployment

- No new required Railway variables.
- No Supabase SQL migration.
- Existing v6.8 environment variables remain valid.
