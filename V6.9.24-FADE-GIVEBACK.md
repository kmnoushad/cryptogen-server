# v6.9.24 — fade profit-giveback guard and read-only trade audit

The fade worker now checks Binance's paginated USDT realized PnL, commission,
and funding records before NEW entries. Within the Dubai calendar day, it
calculates the net realized high-water mark. Once net profit was at least the
greater of $3 or 2% of current equity, giving back the greater of $2 or 1%
of equity blocks further entries until the next Dubai day. The existing 2%
daily loss limit and two-loss cooldown remain. Deposits and withdrawals are
excluded from the net result. Open PnL is excluded: this rule does not close
an existing position and cannot guarantee profits or prevent gaps. The worker
continues reconciling existing positions while the gate is shut.

On AWS, the read-only report can examine seven days of actual trade-related
cash flows and journaled fade jobs. The report does not acquire a lease, place
orders or print API keys:

```sh
cd /opt/nexio
sudo /usr/bin/node --env-file=/etc/nexio-fade.env scripts/fade-performance.js
```

Share its output, not the environment file or credentials. It reports realized
net high-water mark and drawdown, commission/funding, and bot-owned closed
jobs ranked by result. Exchange income that cannot be verified against a
journaled job is labelled unmatched/open rather than assigned a made-up win.
Previously recorded jobs lack sampled floating-profit peaks. From this version
on, the worker records the best 5-second sampled pre-partial quote-based net
estimate while a position is open. That estimate is not a guaranteed
executable price and it does not capture prices between samples.

Merge updates Railway automatically if configured, but AWS does not auto-pull.
Only update AWS after checking active positions and native stops; the old
worker can keep protecting positions while code is fetched and dependencies
are installed. Run the read-only preflight, then restart the AWS service.
Confirm the worker's startup version, fresh reconciliation, and Binance
positions/orders. The calendar/OI/funding gates and existing live
acknowledgements remain in force. Historical results and this guard do not
establish a profitable strategy; any new trading method requires a separate
out-of-sample assessment before live use.
