# NEXIO v6.6 changes

## Futures silence root cause

The production BTC gate required three exact conditions at once: BTC above the hourly EMA50, EMA50 above EMA200, and EMA50 higher than it was six hours earlier. Missing either comparison by even 0.001% disabled every Futures symbol.

A live six-hour replay on 9 August 2026 found 40+ alt impulse setups, 30+ healthy retests and multiple price/flow FIRE completions. Most qualifying observations were discarded at the binary BTC gate before they could arm. The retest/reclaim state machine itself completed valid signals in the replay.

## Correction

- The gate now uses the latest closed five-minute BTC price, not the older hourly close, against the hourly EMA structure.
- `BULLISH` remains the normal full-trend state.
- `BULLISH_RETEST` is also entry-eligible only when EMA50 remains above EMA200, BTC is within 0.35% below EMA50, the EMA50 six-hour slope is at least -0.05%, the five-minute micro-trend is healthy, and no shock condition is active.
- A deeper EMA50 loss, meaningful EMA deterioration, bearish micro-trend, or downside shock still blocks all new Futures entries.
- Manipulation, liquidity, OI, spread, depth, retest, reclaim, R:R and account-risk gates are unchanged.

## Verification and diagnostics

- `/status` and `/diagnostics` now show BTC distance from EMA50 and the EMA50 six-hour slope.
- `npm run smoke` validates the live Binance universe and sample market-data path. Its missing universe configuration fields were fixed.
- `npm run audit:futures` replays six hours of closed one-minute data through the price/flow candidate state machine and reports raw versus BTC-gated results. It intentionally does not pretend that present-day order-book depth reconstructs historical depth.
- No SQL migration is required from v6.4 or v6.5.
- The two new Railway variables are optional. If absent, the safe defaults above are used.
