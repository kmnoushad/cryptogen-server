# NEXIO v6.7 changes

## Confirmed cause of Alpha-only output

Alpha and Futures were already separate pipelines and tables. Alpha wrote to `nexio_alpha_trades` and sent `[ALPHA]`; Futures wrote to `nexio_trades` and could only send `[FUTURES]`. The missing Futures alerts were not mislabeled Alpha messages.

The production diagnostics exposed two interacting Futures blockers:

1. BTC could be above a rising hourly EMA50/EMA200 structure and still become `NO_LONG_EDGE` because a short five-minute micro-trend condition was mandatory.
2. The Futures universe excluded ETH, BNB, SOL, XRP, ADA and TRX. The remaining fast low-cap contracts could satisfy momentum but then correctly failed the $100k-per-side depth gate. Deep contracts moved more slowly and rarely satisfied the low-cap breakout pattern.

## Corrections

- Added `BULLISH_PULLBACK`: BTC remains long-eligible above the rising hourly structure during a bounded intraday pullback. Five-minute, 15-minute and one-hour downside limits and the existing shock block remain enforced.
- Restored deep non-meme Binance USDT perpetuals to the Futures universe. BTC remains excluded because it is the market-regime instrument.
- Added `LIQUID_TREND` detection for slower, deep contracts.
- Anchored a liquid-trend retest near EMA20/support rather than falsely requiring the older high to hold through every pullback.
- Requires the 15-minute/30-minute move and three-minute/15-minute taker flow to remain positive at the final liquid reclaim. A single green one-minute candle cannot revive a setup whose broader momentum has faded.
- Kept all execution and safety requirements: closed candles, later retest/reclaim, taker flow, OI, funding/manipulation assessment, configured depth and spread, structural stop and net R:R.
- Added setup-specific armed and FIRE counters to `/diagnostics`.
- Made `/version` and startup messages explicitly label `[FUTURES]` and `[ALPHA]` as separate paths.

## Verification

The live closed-candle audit on 9 August 2026 classified BTC as `BULLISH_PULLBACK` instead of the incorrect `NO_LONG_EDGE`. In the final 24-hour replay, the corrected price/flow path produced 169 arms, 117 retests and 12 technical completions. The final persistence check removed deep HYPE/NEAR examples that had already lost broader momentum and subsequently stopped. A sustained KAITO price/flow example reached TP1, but its later order-book snapshot fell just below the configured ask-depth floor; the bot correctly keeps that execution gate rather than lowering it to manufacture alerts.

The replay demonstrates that Futures candidates can now arm and advance while BTC is in a controlled bullish pullback. Historical order-book depth cannot be reconstructed from current snapshots, so replay completions are not presented as real issued trades. A live `[FUTURES]` alert still requires contemporaneous depth, spread, OI, funding/manipulation and account-risk approval. Keep `PAPER_MODE=true` and judge only database-monitored outcomes.

No SQL migration and no new Railway variable are required from v6.4–v6.6.
