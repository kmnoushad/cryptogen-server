# NEXIO v6.8 changes

## Audit findings verified against the code

Two reported defects were already protected in v6.7:

- The 1.60% maximum stop is a rejection ceiling. The engine calculates the structural stop first and cancels the setup when it is wider than the ceiling; it never forces that stop inward to 1.60%.
- A reclaim was already anchored to the stored breakout/support level. It required the level to hold and then required a later closed candle to close above the previous candle high.

Those protections remain unchanged and now have explicit regression tests.

## New protections

- Added a parallel Binance USD-M BTC aggregate-trade WebSocket guard. A rolling 0.35% fall from the ten-second peak immediately cancels every pending Futures candidate and holds the new-entry gate for two minutes.
- Added automatic WebSocket reconnect, health reporting and `/status` visibility. If the stream reconnects, the existing REST/closed-candle BTC gate continues to operate.
- Anchored every armed candidate to the low of its original impulse wave. More than a 50% giveback rejects the setup before any green bounce can qualify.
- Replaced fixed entry-slippage simulation with a live ask-ladder walk for `ASSUMED_ORDER_NOTIONAL_USD` (default $1,000). The signal uses the estimated average fill and rejects incomplete or greater-than-8-bps fills.
- Added final-entry aggregated 0.5% bid/ask imbalance, bid-depth retention and spread-expansion checks. Top-three imbalance is recorded for diagnostics but is not a standalone permission because near-touch orders are easy to spoof.
- Added `[FUTURES] BTC REALTIME SHOCK` notification and a persisted `FUTURES_REALTIME_SHOCK` event. It reports how many pending entries were cancelled.

## Deployment

Replace the complete project, including `package.json` and `package-lock.json`; Railway will install the `ws` package automatically. No Supabase migration and no required Railway variable are added. The new optional variables and their safe defaults are in `.env.example`.

Keep `PAPER_MODE=true`. This update improves the validity and speed of the gates; it does not prove that the strategy has positive expectancy. Promotion still requires a frozen, out-of-sample paper sample.
