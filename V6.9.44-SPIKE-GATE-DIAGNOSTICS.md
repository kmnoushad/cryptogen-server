# v6.9.44 spike gate diagnostics

Earlier health text printed only the latest reason emitted by whichever symbol last updated. In a several-hundred-symbol stream, that could say "Warming up" while hundreds of other symbols were ready. It did not prove that all symbols were warming or report which gate most often withheld candidates.

The health line now counts, across the full configured universe:

- **ready**: active trade stream, at least 140 seconds of retained history, currently receiving events;
- **warming**: recent events but less than the required trade history;
- **quiet**: no trade event for five seconds; its baseline resets if a new event resumes after a gap over five seconds;
- **unseen**: no valid trade event has initialized per-symbol detector state;
- **WATCH**: a fresh spike is waiting for rejection;
- **top gate**: the most common latest rejection among ready symbols, counted once per symbol rather than once per tick.

No entry thresholds were changed in this update. Use these counts before deciding whether to loosen the 2%/30-second spike and 3x-volume burst filters. A coin meeting a 24-hour or older pump condition does not mean it meets these faster conditions. Lowering a trigger before measuring its rejection/stop outcome increases entries but supplies no evidence that expected net value improved.

This is telemetry, not a profitability claim. Quiet and unseen counts show coverage gaps; feed connection count alone only establishes open sockets. Existing detector thresholds and AWS order route remain the same.
