# v6.9.15 — /market context report

Use `/market` (alias `/mood`) to request current BTC and liquid-altcoin direction,
derivatives positioning and upcoming high-impact US financial-event risks.
The command is owner-only and informational. It opens no trades, changes no
gates and does not send periodic market predictions. Existing alert logic is unchanged.

## Direction and data quality

- BULLISH: fresh BTC 15m score >=20 and 30m score >0, at least 60% of valid
  altcoins rising over closed 15m, and median return >=+0.10%.
- BEARISH: BTC 15m score <=-20 and 30m score <0, no more than 40% rising, and
  median return <=-0.10%.
- Otherwise MIXED / SIDEWAYS when data are sufficient, or INSUFFICIENT DATA.

These are heuristic descriptions of agreement, not calibrated probabilities or
forecasts. The report omits the old BTC heuristic confidence percentage to avoid
presenting it as a probability of future success.

The independent breadth sampler reuses the existing liquidity selection and
closed-bar return logic: up to 30 eligible non-meme liquid altcoins, at least
20 valid and 80% coverage. Falling breadth is valid evidence, not missing data.
BTC snapshots expire after 180 seconds; breadth observations after 90 seconds,
bars after 330 seconds and liquidity metadata after ten minutes. Selection is
refreshed every five minutes when requested. Requests share one refresh and are
throttled to once a minute; there is no new background polling loop.

Taker flow, funding and OI are BTC positioning proxies from the existing bias
engine. Social/news sentiment and Fear & Greed are not connected. Funding
extremes are context, not automatic directional calls. Components unavailable
from the bias engine are explicitly shown as unavailable.

## Event risk

The existing Finnhub calendar supplies the next five events within 72 hours
plus releases in the last 15 minutes, displayed in GST. A release within one
hour or in the last 15 minutes marks elevated event risk, without changing the
direction assessment. Calendar data are considered stale after the configured
refresh interval plus 15 minutes, capped at 24 hours. Disabled/missing-key,
HTTP401, failed and stale calendars show UNKNOWN risk. Cached events may still
be listed, explicitly unverified. An empty failed calendar never means all-clear.

The command requests a calendar refresh through its existing throttle, without
sending reminders itself. It does not fix an invalid Finnhub credential or an
account lacking calendar access. Actual releases versus expectations and their
price reactions are not evaluated. No new keys or migrations are required.
