# v6.9.14 — informational pump-fade warnings

Adds a Telegram warning, not a short-entry signal. It never creates a trade,
changes an existing position or consumes the virtual-$100 account's trade slots.
Existing long strategy and paper recovery behavior are unchanged by this addition.

`ENABLE_PUMP_FADE_ALERTS` defaults to true. Set false to disable it. Startup,
`/version`, `/status`, `/why` and HTTP health expose the warning radar. `/pause`
pauses it. It operates independently of the BTC long gate, fast-mover toggle,
event-entry gate and risk-account limits because it only reports observed risk.

Every minute, scan up to the 20 most liquid eligible USDT perpetual gainers
with at least +8% over 24 hours and $15M quote volume. Exclude BTC, the existing
Futures blacklist and meme/other non-coin metadata. There is no maximum-gain
filter. Contracts outside this bounded sample are not monitored by this feature.
Exchange metadata is cached for ten minutes. At most one poll runs at once.

The pattern uses 60–90 consecutive closed one-minute bars and requires:

- A local rise of at least 5% from the preceding low to the first high.
- Two rejected pivot highs within the last 30 bars, separated by at least three
  minutes and a dip of at least 0.3%. Two subsequent closed bars confirm each
  pivot; the second must be within the last ten minutes.
- Second high between 0.5% below and 0.2% above the first. Neither peak candle
  closes within 0.2% of its high, and no later high exceeds the first by 0.2%.
- Last-three-minute taker-buy share at most 48%, down at least five percentage
  points versus the previous 15 minutes.
- Price down over three minutes and latest close below both preceding bars' lows.
- Current closed price between 0.3% and 4% below the highs; avoid late warnings
  after a large decline. Missing, malformed, nonconsecutive or stale bars fail closed.

Warnings explain the evidence, resistance level and what would weaken the
warning. They explicitly say that a fall is not guaranteed. No success-rate
claim or probability is attached: these are experimental heuristic thresholds.

Limit six delivered warnings per rolling hour and one per symbol per 30 minutes
within a process. The event key includes symbol and first-peak close time, so a
persisted pattern is not resent after restart. Process cooldowns/caps reset on
restart. A database failure suppresses delivery and is exposed in health. Events
are reserved before delivery; a delivery failure can leave a reserved event
without a Telegram warning. This favors avoiding duplicates over guaranteed delivery.
No new database tables or API keys are needed.
