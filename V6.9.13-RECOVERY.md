# v6.9.13: paper-only BTC recovery experiment

The previous status proved that BTC gating stopped entries, but did not show
which condition failed. `/status`, `/why`, `/btc` and `/diagnostics` now report
the hourly EMA50/EMA200 relationship, BTC returns and gate failure reasons.
An already-running manual scan is no longer reported as a completed zero-symbol scan.

## Recovery route

Only active with `PAPER_MODE=true`, `PAPER_100_TEST=true` and
`ENABLE_PAPER_BTC_RECOVERY=true` (the new flag defaults to true).
Set the last flag to false to disable this experiment without deleting history.
There are no Binance orders and no additional credentials or migrations required.

When the existing BTC route fails, `BULLISH_RECOVERY` can permit evaluation if:

- BTC is above hourly EMA50 with a positive six-hour EMA50 slope.
- BTC is above five-minute EMA20; 5m/15m/1h returns exceed -0.50/-0.25/-0.60%.
- Closed BTC bars are fresh and no BTC shock/data block is present.
- The realtime shock guard is enabled, connected, current within 30 seconds,
  and outside its shock cooldown.
- At least 20 liquid altcoins have valid common-window 15-minute returns,
  coverage is at least 80%, at least 60% are rising, and median return is at
  least +0.10%.

The breadth sample is the top 30 eligible non-meme USDT perpetual altcoins by
24-hour quote volume (minimum $15 million or the higher configured minimum).
Selection does not use positive returns or the trading universe's gain ceiling.
Strength uses four consecutive closed five-minute candles, never 24-hour gains.
Sampling is throttled to once per minute; samples expire after 90 seconds,
closed bars after 330 seconds and the liquidity universe after ten minutes.
Missing data and failed requests cannot count as advancing coins.

The original BTC route, symbol selection, retest/reclaim, volume/flow, extension,
execution-book, event, risk-sizing and partial/runner exit checks remain intact.
Recovery is rechecked around asynchronous entry work, including before FIRE.
Passing the market gate is not an instruction to trade: a valid setup is still required.

New entries record `setup.marketGateModel` and the BTC regime/breadth snapshot.
Existing paper history is retained; separate recovery entries when comparing
results. These thresholds are experimental, not a validated profitable edge.
Closed-bar simulation still excludes funding and intrabar equity drawdown.
