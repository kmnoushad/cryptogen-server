# v6.9.43 full spike universe correction

The v6.9.42 source incorrectly narrowed the user's spike-search request to the top 40 contracts with $15m daily turnover. This update removes both the top-40 cap and the daily-turnover prefilter.

The source now subscribes to all exchangeInfo contracts with TRADING status, USDT quote, PERPETUAL type and COIN underlying type (or unspecified type), excluding BTC from short candidates. BTC remains subscribed for context. This includes smaller/newer coin perpetuals and meme contracts; it does not identify fraud or establish a profitable short. The displayed count is the actual eligible Binance contract count and is not hard-coded to 700. Stock/index contracts, other quote assets and halted contracts are excluded.

Subscriptions are divided into groups of at most 100 coin symbols, with separate market aggregate-trade and public book-ticker sockets. BTC trades are subscribed once. The stream status reports connected/required sockets; entries require the complete feed set. Connection loss clears all unsent patterns and generation authorization. Existing position protection continues independently.

To keep a broad universe feasible, the detector retains approximately 150 one-second flow buckets per symbol, sums every valid trade's quote amount, sell-aggressor quote and count, and evaluates at most every 250ms. Spike highs still update on each valid tick. Window boundaries therefore have up to one-second aggregation granularity. This is experimental detection, not evidence of latency or profitability on the target AWS host. Thinly traded coins may never complete the continuous-trade warmup: a gap over five seconds still resets the pattern. Freshness, spread, exchange minima, modeled costs, BTC, event/OI/funding and portfolio risk gates remain.

Validation includes a synthetic 740-contract coverage/sharding check and a 10,000-trade aggregation/storage bound test. No exchange orders were placed during development. Use the updated read-only spike smoke script to verify all required sockets and fresh quotes on AWS before resuming entries.

Deployment: pause new entries with /fadepause. In the existing isolated `/opt/nexio-fade-v6.9.42` worktree, fetch the deployment branch and fast-forward its detached HEAD to the tested v6.9.43 commit. Do not reset or overwrite local modifications. Run dependencies, preflight and read-only stream smoke checks. Then restart only `nexio-fade`, check `/fadeauto`, and resume new entries. The systemd path and `FADE_ENTRY_SOURCE=spike` setting remain unchanged.
