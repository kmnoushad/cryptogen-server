# v6.9.27 open fade setup proximity gate

After three consecutive reconciliation quotes with the short at least 75% of
the way from entry to its current stop, the worker sends one warning and
temporarily withholds additional fade shorts. The hold clears when the quote
recovers, the job becomes a protected runner, or the position is closed.
Status includes the fresh quote and distance when applicable. Quotes older
than 60 seconds cannot block fresh entries. This does not modify or cancel
the exchange stop and does not place a hedge or an extra exit order.

The rule does not predict price direction or imply an edge. Futures paper
entries remain long-only; short setups need their own signal model and
out-of-sample paper validation before any live use. BTC, event, OI, funding,
and existing risk gates remain active.
