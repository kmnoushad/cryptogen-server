# v6.9.26 fade entry lock duration

The previous seven-day realized loss/giveback lock prevented new short entries
through later BTC regimes. v6.9.26 keeps the seven-day Binance income report
for monitoring, but does not use that number as an entry veto.

New entries still require the BTC bearish check, event/calendar data, OI and
funding checks, fresh quotes, exchange reconciliation, eligible setup, and
position sizing. The 2% net-loss limit and the realized profit giveback lock
last until Dubai midnight. Two verified losses in a row cause a four-hour
cooldown from the latest close. Native position protection is unchanged.

This increases exposure after a losing week. A bearish BTC regime is necessary
for shorts, but it is not evidence a particular signal will profit. Review net
returns after fees before considering any further changes.

The AWS worker needs to pull the merged commit and restart before the rule
changes. A Railway deployment alone does not change execution.
