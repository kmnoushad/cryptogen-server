# v6.9.37 — exit logic, fade stop placement, restored 7-day loss lock

Audit findings (repo history + code review):

- Fade: 103 closed jobs in 7 days, 46W/57L, net -$31.11 (commission alone -$13.63).
  The stop sat ONE TICK BELOW the failed high, so a normal retest of the high
  stopped trades out even when the high never broke.
- Futures: the fixed $5 net target ignored position size. At $30 equity it needed
  an ~18% move (3.5-4.3% at $150+) against stops ~0.5% away, while break-even
  moved the stop at only 0.5R. Wins were near impossible; trades ended as full
  stops or scratches. There was no time exit.
- v6.9.26 removed the rolling seven-day loss lock that had just started working.

Changes:

1. Futures target is 1.5R net of fees/slippage (was fixed $5). Scales with size.
2. Futures break-even moves the stop at +1R (was +0.5R).
3. Futures max hold 4h: older positions are closed (reduce-only, bot-owned qty).
4. Fade stop is 0.3% ABOVE the failed high (was one tick below it). Quantity
   shrinks automatically to keep modeled risk at 0.5% of equity.
5. Rolling seven-day net loss lock restored: new entries stop when 7-day net
   (realized + commission + funding) <= -max($10, 5% equity). Entry-only; open
   positions keep native protection; old losses roll off. Applies to fade and
   futures (shared risk module). Deposits never count.

Not changed / limits:

- Position sizing caps, daily 2% lock, cooldown, native stops: unchanged.
- This removes structural flaws; it does not prove an edge. Validate on testnet
  or with minimum size before scaling. Wider fade stops mean smaller size per
  trade and may skip entries on very small accounts (min notional).
- BTCUSDT cannot trade below roughly $100+ equity (exchange minimum size).

Deploy (AWS/Lightsail): git apply, npm test, restart nexio-futures (and
nexio-fade if installed). Verify no open positions first; native stops remain
on the exchange during a restart.
