The 24-hour read-only diagnostic found 46 Railway fade warnings and 46 AWS
handoffs. The worker's calendar gate was closed because AWS had no working
Finnhub calendar. The BTC short entry gate was independently closed while its
current BTC snapshot was bullish/mixed.

The AWS fade entry guard now uses official BLS release iCalendar and BEA
release dates when Finnhub is unconfigured or returns invalid results. It checks
for upcoming schedule coverage, applies the existing 60-minute pre-release
and 30-minute post-release exclusion, and blocks published FOMC decision
days in 2026 and 2027. If sources are unreachable, incomplete, or outside the
verified Fed schedule years, new entries remain blocked. Existing positions,
stops, and reconciliation continue.

These schedules cover selected scheduled US releases, not unscheduled news,
earnings, or every market-moving announcement. Calendar clears do not imply
a good trade. No scanner thresholds, BTC short gate, other risk gates, order
sizes, or Railway/Alpha logic changed.

    sudo /usr/bin/node --env-file=/etc/nexio-fade.env scripts/fade-diagnostics.js
