# 6.9.32: New York Fed calendar fallback

AWS receives HTTP 403 from the BLS iCalendar feed. If Finnhub and BLS cannot
verify upcoming releases, the worker now fetches the current and next monthly
economic indicator calendars published by the Federal Reserve Bank of New York.
It extracts only explicitly timed BLS-linked releases, verifies a future
Employment Situation or CPI release within 45 days, and combines them with
the working BEA JSON schedule. The established 60-minute pre-release and
30-minute post-release blocks and published FOMC decision-day blocks remain.

Missing pages, month labels, timetable cells, future coverage, or BEA coverage
still block new entries. Calendar entries are tentative and can change; this
does not cover unscheduled news. The BTC gate and pump-fade scanner eligibility
remain independent. Existing positions and their protection are unaffected.

After pulling main on AWS, run the read-only fade diagnostic before restarting.
The calendar result must identify NYFed/BEA or another verified source as OPEN,
or an event window as CLOSED. The diagnostic now reports access and parse
results for the New York Fed pages when BLS returns 403. Do not claim the
fallback is operational on AWS until this check succeeds there.
